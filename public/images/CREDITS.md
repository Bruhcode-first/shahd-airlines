## Map data

`public/data/countries-110m.geojson` (the globe's land dots and the flat route map) is derived from Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 countries, public domain (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/). Properties were stripped, coordinates rounded to 0.01 degrees, Antarctica and degenerate rings removed.
